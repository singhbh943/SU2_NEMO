NEMO MUTATION++ AIR11 PRESSURE-DERIVATIVE HARDENING
====================================================

Parent:
7b27e34dd73ee1b11c3fcaff07e60c8098f25f79

Scope
-----
This change is limited to the Mutation++ NEMO thermodynamic
pressure derivative used by the implicit Jacobian.

The native CNEMOGas::ComputedPdU() implementation body was not changed.

Problem
-------
NEMO pressure for an ionized two-temperature mixture is evaluated as

  p = sum_e rho_s (Ru/M_s) Tve
    + sum_h rho_s (Ru/M_s) T

but the inherited CNEMOGas::ComputedPdU() closed-form derivative was
not consistent with Mutation++ ChemNonEqTTv's two-mode energy
definition.

Before the fix, an AIR11 conservative finite-difference regression
showed large pressure-derivative errors while dT/dU and dTve/dU
remained accurate.

Observed pre-fix examples:
  relative dP/dU error ~0.35--0.72
  pressure derivative failures = 13

Implementation
--------------
CNEMOGas::ComputedPdU() was made virtual.

CMutationTCLib supplies a Mutation++-specific override using

  dp/dU_j =
      rhoR_heavy    * dT/dU_j
    + rhoR_electron * dTve/dU_j
    + direct species-density pressure contribution

where the temperature derivatives are the already validated
Mutation++-consistent derivatives.
